What would actually help?

We want to learn from people directly affected by digital access challenges. Please avoid names, private contacts and sensitive details in feedback.

6–12 month proposal

Discover → Co-design → Prototype → Pilot → Improve

Months 1–2

Interview affected users and document the real problem.

Months 3–5

Co-design and test an accessible minimum viable prototype.

Months 6–12

Run a small pilot, measure usefulness and improve from evidence.

Proposed measures: completed profiles, useful enquiries, user-reported usefulness, response time and accessibility issues fixed. No impact results are claimed yet.
