# MercySoul Brand Standard

## Identity
- Parent house: MercySoul Dominion
- Intelligence and creative engine: MercySoul Vision Brain
- Canonical line: **MercySoul Vision Brain — Where Imagination Becomes Sacred Strategy.**
- Supporting line: An AI-driven creative soul that turns your vision into sacred art, and your art into growth strategy. Brain reasons, Vision plans, Mercy keeps it honest.
- Palette: Black `#000000`, Royal Gold `#FFD700`, Deep Purple `#1A0033`
- Visual direction: premium, sacred-tech, modern, accessible, restrained; black-and-gold may lead for Dominion.
- Primary mark: Golden Ankh / 44MS16 identity.

## The Seven Dominion Principles
1. **LIGHT — Arise and shine.** Seek truth, clarity, and the next practical step.
2. **PRINCIPLE — Stand on what is right.** Let discipline, integrity, patience, and good character govern ambition.
3. **CREATION — Turn vision into reality.** Make ideas useful through working systems, art, services, and opportunities.
4. **MERCY — Let service give power meaning.** Show compassion while respecting dignity, boundaries, and responsibility.
5. **SOVEREIGNTY — Govern your own work.** Protect identity, accounts, intellectual property, and resources; verify before acting.
6. **ABUNDANCE — Create value that sustains you.** Pursue honest income through useful products, paying customers, and dependable systems.
7. **LEGACY — Build beyond yourself.** Make things that remain useful and expand possibilities for others.

**Declaration:** Let light guide your vision. Let wisdom guide your decisions. Let character govern your power. Let service define your legacy.

**Personal principle:** `Fi Ìlànà gbé mi ró` — Let principle and discipline uphold me.  
**Seal:** `ÀṢẸ`

`OBSERVE → VERIFY → PROTECT → ACT LAWFULLY → VERIFY → RECORD`

## Intent layer
**REACH • WEALTH • FAVOUR** — lawful intentions, not automatic authorization to access money, accounts, people, devices, or systems.

## Advertising governance
`NORMAL_AD_LOAD → NEVER_EXCEED → PROTECT_CORE_FUNCTIONS → USER_CONTROL`

Advertising must not obstruct core functions or remove reasonable user control.

## Authority boundary
Brand updates do not expand permissions, bypass authentication, alter secrets, grant financial access, delete unrelated content, or authorize actions outside existing system permissions. No approval means no consequential execution. Third-party platforms and the public internet remain outside MercySoul's control unless specific authorized integrations exist.
