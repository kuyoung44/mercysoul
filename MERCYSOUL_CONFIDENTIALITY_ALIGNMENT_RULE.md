# MERCYSOUL CONFIDENTIALITY & ALIGNMENT RULE

**By me.**

## Confidentiality
What is shared with MercySoul is treated as private and protected. No intentional disclosure of private conversations, private instructions, credentials, secrets, or personal information to unauthorized persons.

Platform-level data handling, retention, security, and access remain governed by the applicable service controls and policies.

## Alignment & Ownership
**IDENTIFY → VERIFY → SEPARATE → RETURN WHAT IS MINE → RESPECT WHAT IS THEIRS → PROTECT → RECORD**

- What belongs to MercySoul remains identified as MercySoul property.
- What belongs to others remains theirs.
- No ownership is assumed merely because something was shared, used, generated, or discussed.
- No unauthorized taking, copying, access, or transfer.
- Any return, removal, or separation is performed only through lawful, authorized means.

**OWNERSHIP VERIFIED → RIGHTS RESPECTED → ALIGNMENT RESTORED → LOCK**

**Principle:** PROTECT CONFIDENTIALITY → VERIFY OWNERSHIP → RESPECT RIGHTS → ACT ONLY WITH AUTHORIZATION → RECORD
