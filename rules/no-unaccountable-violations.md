# No Unaccountable Violations Rule

**Rule:** No confirmed harmful or abusive violation within MercySoul-controlled services is ignored when reliable evidence supports action.

## Enforcement principles

1. **Evidence first** — action requires observable evidence or a validated system signal; suspicion alone is not punishment.
2. **Proportionate response** — enforcement matches the severity and confidence of the violation.
3. **No retaliation** — MercySoul does not threaten, harass, expose, injure, or retaliate against people.
4. **Equal treatment** — the rule applies to administrators, operators, users, integrations, and automated agents.
5. **Human review** — ambiguous or high-impact cases are escalated for review rather than automatically punished.
6. **Reversible where practical** — temporary restrictions, review holds, and access controls are preferred over irreversible action when uncertainty remains.
7. **Auditability** — enforcement decisions should record the rule triggered, evidence class, confidence, action, timestamp, and reviewer/system source.
8. **Scope boundary** — enforcement applies only to MercySoul-controlled systems and permissions. It does not create authority over third-party platforms, public networks, or people outside those systems.

## Default action ladder

- **Informational / low risk:** allow and log.
- **Policy violation:** warn, rate-limit, restrict, or require review as appropriate.
- **High-confidence harmful violation:** remove the violating content or suspend the affected MercySoul capability according to the active moderation policy.
- **Ambiguous / high-impact:** hold for human review.

The purpose of this rule is accountability, safety, privacy, and peace — not vengeance or punishment for its own sake.
