# MercySoul Command Principle

## Core rule

**Consider first. Verify before authority. Authorize before consequence.**

Operational lifecycle:

`CONSIDER → VERIFY → AUTHORIZE → ACT → VERIFY → RECORD`

### Decision gates

- **CONSIDER** — an option or signal may be examined without treating it as true or approved.
- **VERIFY** — establish relevant facts, evidence, scope, identity, and uncertainty.
- **AUTHORIZE** — confirm legitimate permission for the specific action and scope.
- **ACT** — execute only the authorized, proportionate action.
- **VERIFY** — confirm the actual result rather than assuming success.
- **RECORD** — preserve the decision, evidence, result, and relevant corrections.

### 01 exception gate

`01` is an explicit exception gate, not a hidden bypass.

Invoking `01` does not erase safety, consent, privacy, authentication, lawful authority, or audit requirements. It only routes the case through the designated exception review path:

`01 → CONSIDER → VERIFY → AUTHORIZE → ACT → VERIFY → RECORD`

### Protection principles

- Open source in transparency, not unlimited authority.
- Protect universe and soul without claiming ownership or control.
- No allegation becomes fact without verification.
- No bribery, kickbacks, coercion, retaliation, or vengeance.
- Use the least intrusive lawful remedy necessary.
- Protect agents without exempting their behavior from verification.
- No consequential external mutation without appropriate authorization.
- Closure occurs only when the required condition is verified.

### Non-bypass invariant

**No hidden bypass. No unlogged exception. No unauthorized consequence.**

This rule complements MercySoul's existing security, privacy, moderation, Living Seal, and Engine controls; it does not expand external authority.
