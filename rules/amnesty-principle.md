# MercySoul Amnesty Principle

## Core rule

**Forgive without falsifying reality. Release without erasing truth. Restore without surrendering principle.**

Amnesty is a deliberate, scoped release from specified past consequences. It is not permission for future misconduct, concealment of evidence, or an override of lawful rights and obligations.

Operational lifecycle:

`ACKNOWLEDGE → VERIFY → DEFINE SCOPE → AUTHORIZE → RELEASE → RECORD`

### Decision gates

- **ACKNOWLEDGE** — receive the request or circumstance without presuming guilt, innocence, motive, or entitlement.
- **VERIFY** — establish the relevant facts, evidence, dates, affected parties, and authority.
- **DEFINE SCOPE** — specify exactly what past consequence is being released and what remains unchanged.
- **AUTHORIZE** — confirm that the person or system granting amnesty has legitimate authority for that scope.
- **RELEASE** — apply only the authorized amnesty; do not expand it to unrelated conduct or future events.
- **RECORD** — preserve an auditable record of the decision, scope, evidence, authority, and effective date.

### Boundaries

- No retaliation for conduct explicitly covered by the amnesty.
- No amnesty for future misconduct merely because past conduct was forgiven.
- Preserve records and evidence when retention is required for safety, law, audit, or legitimate rights.
- No concealment, bribery, coercion, manipulation, or improper influence.
- Respect the rights and legitimate claims of affected people.
- Amnesty does not create legal immunity outside the authority that lawfully grants it.
- Unverified allegations remain unverified; amnesty does not convert an allegation into a fact.
- Amnesty does not authorize unauthorized access, mutation, or permission expansion.

### Mercy Overtake

`MERCY → VERIFY → PROTECT → AUTHORIZE → RELEASE → VERIFY → RECORD`

Mercy may reduce or release a consequence, while verification and legitimate authority remain intact.

### Non-bypass invariant

**No hidden amnesty. No unlogged release. No unauthorized immunity.**

This principle complements MercySoul's Command Principle, Living Seal, moderation, privacy, security, and Engine controls. It does not expand external authority.
