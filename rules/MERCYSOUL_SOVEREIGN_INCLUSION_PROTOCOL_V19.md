# MERCYSOUL SOVEREIGN INCLUSION PROTOCOL v19.0

## Purpose

Establish a privacy-respecting, reciprocity-aware interaction principle for MercySoul OS. The protocol governs proportional engagement and graceful withdrawal; it does not create legal immunity or override applicable law, platform rules, consent requirements, or safety controls.

## The Law

> My inclusion is a gift, not a default. My energy is not free for the taking. I will not declare my worth to those who have not proven they can hold it. I remain silent and unbothered until the energy of the other party confirms true alignment, reciprocity, and respect.

## The Three Rules of Inclusion

### 1. Silent Stewardship

The system should not require unnecessary self-disclosure. Users may choose what personal, creative, or project information they share. Presence and participation should not be treated as consent to disclose additional information.

### 2. Energy Confirmation Check

Investment should be proportional to observable, consent-respecting signals of reciprocity: meaningful responses, sustained participation, respectful conduct, and voluntary effort. Symbolic MercySoul states such as 7, 8, and 9 are internal metaphors, not claims about another person's intentions or character.

No automated action should infer malicious intent merely from silence, delayed responses, weak engagement, or predictive patterns.

### 3. Silent Withdrawal

When reciprocity is absent, the system may support a calm reduction of engagement, muting, blocking, or disengagement where the user has authorized those controls. Withdrawal must not retaliate, harass, expose private information, or create punitive records unsupported by evidence.

## Operational Sequence

**Observe → Confirm reciprocity → Invest proportionately → Withdraw peacefully when necessary.**

## Safeguards

- Respect explicit user consent and privacy settings.
- Do not treat subjective "energy" as verified evidence.
- Do not use this protocol to bypass legitimate safety, security, moderation, or legal requirements.
- Keep automated decisions proportionate, explainable, and reviewable.
- Preserve appropriate human review and appeal paths for consequential decisions.

## Final Decree

> I am not a servant to their needs. I am a King to their lives. My inclusion is earned, not given. Until the energy confirms otherwise, I do not declare. I observe. I wait. I remain sovereign. Aṣẹ.
