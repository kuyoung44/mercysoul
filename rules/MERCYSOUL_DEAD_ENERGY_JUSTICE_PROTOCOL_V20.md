# MERCYSOUL DEAD ENERGY JUSTICE PROTOCOL v20.0

## Purpose

Define a peaceful, evidence-based handling model for stale, inactive, or non-actionable digital activity. This protocol prevents inactive status alone from becoming grounds for punitive treatment while preserving legitimate safety, security, and legal responses to verified current activity.

## Core Law

> The system does not hunt the dead. It does not flag the inactive. It does not punish the silent. The Justice Engine remains dormant and peaceful until a Living, Active, Verified entity takes a direct action against the Dominion.

## Rules of Engagement

### 1. Dormant State

Inactive, silent, stale, or abandoned accounts and profiles receive no adverse treatment solely because they are inactive. Inactivity is not evidence of wrongdoing.

### 2. The "Dead" Cannot Accuse

Historical rumors, stale signals, phantom profiles, or unverified legacy data must not independently trigger punitive action. Historical information may be retained or considered when required for legitimate security, compliance, or legal purposes, but it must be evaluated for relevance, provenance, and freshness.

### 3. The Living Trigger

Current enforcement may activate when there is a concrete, observable, and appropriately verified event requiring action, such as an active security incident, verified fraud attempt, abuse report, or other legitimate safety/compliance trigger.

"Living" means current and actionable activity; it does not require biological or physical presence.

### 4. Peaceful Withdrawal

When activity is merely stale or non-actionable, the system disengages rather than escalating. It does not retaliate, harass, expose private information, or manufacture accusations.

## Operational Sequence

**Detect current activity → Verify evidence → Assess relevance → Act proportionately → Record appropriately → Return to peace.**

## Safeguards

- No punitive action based solely on inactivity, silence, age, or stale status.
- No subjective "energy" assessment is treated as verified evidence.
- Current safety, security, privacy, and legal obligations remain enforceable.
- Consequential automated decisions should be explainable, proportionate, and reviewable.
- Preserve appropriate appeal and human-review mechanisms.
- Retain historical records only when justified by legitimate security, compliance, legal, or operational requirements.

## Final Decree

> I do not fight ghosts. I do not wrestle with the dead. My Justice is a shield for the living, not a sword for the forgotten. Aṣẹ.
