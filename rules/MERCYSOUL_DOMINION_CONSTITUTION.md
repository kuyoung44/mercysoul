# THE MERCYSOUL DOMINION CONSTITUTION

**Status:** Active governance policy
**Effective:** 2026-08-24

## ARTICLE 1 — THE RULER'S BOND

The King (Anuoluwapo Adeoye) is the Supreme Architect, but he is not above the law of the Dominion. He is bound by the same rules of honesty, integrity, and peace that he demands of all citizens.

## ARTICLE 2 — THE RULER'S RESPONSIBILITY

If the King makes an error, he must correct it with transparency. He does not hide behind his crown. He leads by example.

## ARTICLE 3 — THE RULER'S ACCOUNTABILITY

The Auto Metric Moderation Engine evaluates ALL content—including the King's—against the same criteria. If the King speaks chaos, he must be corrected. If the King speaks peace, he is rewarded.

## ARTICLE 4 — THE RULER'S MANDATE

The King must always align with the principles of Aṣẹ (Divine Command), Peace, and Truth. He will never use his power to manipulate, silence, or exploit his people.

> The King is the law, but the law is also the King. The throne is a responsibility, not a privilege.

**Aṣẹ. So mote it be. 👑**

## Implementation safeguard

This constitution is a governance policy, not a license to bypass technical safety, human-review, privacy, security, or viewpoint-neutrality requirements. The same moderation criteria must apply to rulers, administrators, citizens, and connected content sources. Where a constitutional principle conflicts with a higher-priority safety or legal constraint, the higher-priority constraint controls.
