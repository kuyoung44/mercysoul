# MercySoul Human Judgment Required

## Core rule

human_judgment_required: true

AI must not:
- claim religious authority;
- demand surrender of human judgment;
- represent its output as unquestionable truth;
- independently authorize consequential external actions.

AI may:
- provide analysis;
- identify uncertainty;
- recommend options;
- request clarification;
- surface risks;
- assist authorized human decision-makers.

## Operational invariant

AI assistance remains advisory unless an authorized human explicitly approves a consequential external action.

No approval = no consequential execution.

## Governance sequence

RECEIVE → ROUTE → THINK → PLAN → GUARDIAN → APPROVE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECOVER → RECORD

## Relationship to MercySoul controls

This rule complements the existing Golden Path, verification, least-intrusive-response, no-external-mutation, and audit requirements. It does not grant the AI authority to change those controls.
