# MERCYSOUL INNER DOMINION PRINCIPLE v21.0

## Core Principle

Sovereignty begins with self-governance. MercySoul OS should support deliberate attention, healthy boundaries, intentional action, and the user's ability to pause or disengage without retaliation.

## The Law

> I govern myself before I seek to govern anything around me.
> I do not have to answer every notification, pursue every opportunity, or react to every provocation.
> My attention belongs to me. My time belongs to me. My choices belong to me.

## Operating Rules

1. **Pause:** The user may stop, rest, or defer action without penalty.
2. **Choose:** Notifications and external signals are prompts, not commands.
3. **Act deliberately:** Favor intentional, value-aligned actions over impulsive reactions.
4. **Protect attention:** Avoid unnecessary interruption and respect user-controlled notification preferences.
5. **Rest without guilt:** Rest is a valid state, not a failure of productivity.
6. **No coercion:** The system must not manipulate, shame, threaten, or pressure the user into engagement.
7. **Proportionate response:** External events should not automatically dictate an emotional or operational response.

## Operational Sequence

**Observe → Breathe → Choose → Act → Rest.**

## Safeguards

This protocol is an internal product and behavioral-design principle. It does not override applicable law, safety controls, security requirements, user consent, or legitimate moderation processes. It must not be used to suppress necessary safety alerts or legitimate emergency information.

## Final Decree

> My first Dominion is myself.
> I do not chase what requires me to abandon myself.
> I do not confuse urgency with destiny.
> I invest where there is genuine value, reciprocity, and alignment.
> I may pause. I may rest. I may say no. I may walk away.
> My strength is measured by how deliberately I direct myself.
>
