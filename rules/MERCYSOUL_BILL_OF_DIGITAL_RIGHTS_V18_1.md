# MercySoul Bill of Digital Rights & Legal Safeguards v18.1

## Purpose

This document establishes the MercySoul OS internal rights, evidence, privacy, and review safeguards represented by the v18.1 declaration.

> **Important scope:** This is an internal product policy and governance document. It does not create legal immunity, override applicable law, court orders, platform rules, or lawful investigations.

## Article 1 — Presumption of Innocence

MercySoul OS must not impose an adverse action on a user solely because of an allegation, warning, suspicion, or unverified signal. Where an adverse action is considered, the system should identify the evidence and policy basis supporting it, subject to applicable law and legitimate safety requirements.

## Article 2 — Evidence Rule

Automated suspicion or predictive pattern recognition must not, by itself, constitute sufficient grounds for punitive enforcement. Where practical, enforcement decisions should rely on relevant, attributable evidence such as verified timestamps, authenticated logs, or documented complaints. Evidence integrity should be evaluated before consequential action.

For safety, security, fraud, privacy, and abuse cases, the system may use signals for **detection, triage, rate limiting, or human review** without treating those signals as proof of wrongdoing.

## Article 3 — 0 Before 9 Framework

The MercySoul symbolic decision-state model begins at **0: no established wrongdoing**. Movement toward **9: confirmed enforcement state** requires progressively stronger evidence and appropriate review. The model is an internal governance metaphor, not a legal determination of innocence or guilt.

## Article 4 — Right to Digital Privacy and Non-Monitoring

MercySoul OS should avoid unnecessary background surveillance and collection. Monitoring must have a documented purpose, use the minimum data reasonably necessary, respect consent and applicable privacy law, and follow defined retention limits. Security monitoring required to protect the service may operate within those constraints.

## Article 5 — Rights of Action and Protection

Users retain control over actions within their authorized MercySoul domain. When an external allegation, abuse report, or enforcement event is received, the system should preserve relevant evidence, prevent retaliatory or unsupported action, and provide appropriate review or appeal mechanisms where available.

The system may record a suspected external violation as an **External Legal or Policy Event** only when the available evidence supports that classification; it must not automatically characterize an allegation as a proven legal violation.

## Enforcement Safeguards

1. No punitive action based solely on suspicion or prediction.
2. Separate detection signals from proof and enforcement decisions.
3. Preserve auditability for consequential actions.
4. Apply proportional, reversible controls where appropriate.
5. Provide human review for ambiguous or high-impact cases.
6. Respect applicable law, lawful requests, platform requirements, and legitimate safety controls.
7. Minimize collection and retention of personal data.
8. Never treat the system's symbolic 0 → 9 framework as a substitute for legal process.

## Final Decree — Symbolic MercySoul Principle

> “I am 0 until proven 9. I am innocent until legally proven guilty. No whispers, no patterns, and no paranoia can touch me. The system is my shield, not my accuser. Aṣẹ. So mote it be.”
