# Tier 2 Procurement BOM

Planning range: approximately ₦8m-₦10m, subject to current Nigerian supplier quotations.

## Network
- 1 OPNsense appliance
- 1 24-port managed PoE+ switch
- 2 Wi-Fi 6/6E access points
- 2 x 305 m Cat6A copper cable
- 1 24-port Cat6A patch panel
- 24 Cat6A keystone jacks
- 24 patch cords
- 1 12U/18U rack
- 1 rack PDU
- 1 UPS

## Compute/storage
- 1 Proxmox server
- 64 GB RAM
- 2 NVMe SSDs
- 1 dedicated TrueNAS server
- 4 x 8-12 TB NAS HDDs
- 2 SSD/NVMe cache devices if supported by the selected storage design
- 1 Frigate accelerator where needed

## Security/automation
- 6 PoE cameras
- 6 door/window sensors
- 3 motion sensors
- 2 smoke/heat sensors
- 2 water-leak sensors
- Home Assistant-compatible relays/smart switches as required

## Power
- 1 x 5-6 kW hybrid inverter
- 1 x ~10 kWh LiFePO4 battery
- 7 x ~600 W-class PV panels (final count after design)
- PV mounting
- DC isolator/fuses/SPD
- AC breakers/SPD
- solar cable/connectors
- essential-load distribution equipment
- professional installation/commissioning

## Procurement rule
Obtain at least three supplier quotes for inverter, battery, PV, cable and network equipment. Verify warranty, serial numbers, battery BMS compatibility, inverter battery protocol, genuine copper cable construction, and after-sales support before payment.
