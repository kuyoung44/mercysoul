# MercySoul Public Presence & Confidentiality Policy

**Governing principle:** Fi Ìlànà gbé mi ró — Let principle and discipline uphold me.

## Mission
Make MercySoul Dominion and MercySoul Vision Brain discoverable through truthful public communication, useful creative work, clear product descriptions, and verifiable results.

## Public disclosure
- Publish approved descriptions of products, services, portfolio work, pricing where approved, contact channels, and genuine milestones.
- Use accurate claims. Label prototypes, previews, estimates, and unverified capabilities clearly.
- Do not invent customers, testimonials, revenue, partnerships, security certifications, model capabilities, or deployment status.
- Correct material errors promptly and clearly.

## Confidentiality
- Never publish credentials, API keys, private tokens, personal customer information, private security findings, or non-public source code without explicit authorization.
- Share sensitive implementation details only with authorized recipients on a need-to-know basis.
- Use least-privilege access, secret stores, redaction, and review before publishing logs, screenshots, repositories, or incident reports.
- Do not imply that confidential information is being withheld to deceive people about material facts, risks, or obligations.

## Incident communications
- Distinguish confirmed facts, unverified leads, and unknowns.
- Preserve evidence and provenance; avoid unsupported attribution of intent or guilt.
- Disclose material incidents to affected people and appropriate authorities when legally or ethically required.
- Keep private security details restricted where disclosure would create a credible risk, while remaining truthful about material impact and uncertainty.

## Release checklist
1. Is the content accurate and supported by evidence?
2. Does it expose secrets or personal data?
3. Is the disclosure authorized and proportionate?
4. Could an omission materially mislead its intended audience?
5. Are claims about live systems, integrations, and results independently verified?
6. Has the review decision been recorded?

**Public vision. Protected systems. Verified claims. Controlled disclosure.**

This policy defines publication guidance; it does not itself configure repository visibility, access controls, secret scanning, deployment protection, or production permissions. Those controls must be inspected and verified separately.
