# MercySoul Visual Alignment

## Reference import
The supplied visual references are translated into a reusable UI system rather than copied as opaque assets.

### Visual language
- Deep indigo / cosmic background with soft radial depth.
- Warm gold as the primary accent and evidence/status highlight.
- Sacred-geometry motif: Flower-of-Life-inspired linework.
- Ankh motif as a central symbolic mark.
- Orb / globe composition to represent a connected world of knowledge.
- Orbiting gold light trails to communicate motion, discovery and synthesis.
- Premium dark glass panels with restrained borders and glow.
- High contrast typography; gold is reserved for identity, actions and verified evidence.

## Alignment behavior
The Research Engine should keep the evidence architecture separate from decorative symbolism:

1. **Grounding** — gather current public evidence and source metadata.
2. **Verification** — actively seek primary sources, counter-evidence and corrections.
3. **Synthesis** — combine evidence while preserving uncertainty and disagreement.

Visual identity must never be used as evidence, a confidence score, or a substitute for source quality.

## Asset
`public/mercy-alignment.svg` is the lightweight, repository-native alignment seal used by the Research Engine. It is an original vector interpretation of the supplied visual direction.
