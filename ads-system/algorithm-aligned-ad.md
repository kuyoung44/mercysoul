## Algorithm-aligned creative structure

1. **Stop:** Lead with a concrete visual problem or desire.
2. **Match:** Keep the first sentence tightly aligned with the creative shown.
3. **Value:** Explain the transformation in plain language.
4. **Proof of offer:** State the starting price without exaggerated claims.
5. **Low-friction action:** Ask for one clear next step.
6. **Feedback loop:** Test hook, creative angle, audience and CTA separately.
7. **Optimize for quality:** Prefer qualified conversations, completed orders and revenue over raw impressions or clicks.
8. **Frequency control:** Avoid repetitive delivery that creates ad fatigue.
9. **User control:** Ads must remain clearly distinguishable and never obstruct core product functions.
10. **Truth boundary:** No fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, guaranteed results, deceptive engagement bait, or unsupported performance claims.
