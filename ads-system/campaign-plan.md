# MercySoul Meta Ads Campaign Plan

## Campaign
MERCYSOUL — VISION BRAIN LEAD GENERATION

Objective: Leads  
Monthly test budget: NGN 50,000  
Starting daily budget: approximately NGN 1,600/day  
Primary conversion: qualified lead  
Sales destination: WhatsApp

## Algorithm-aligned optimization

The campaign is designed around relevance and user value rather than engagement bait.

**Signal priority:**
1. Qualified conversations
2. Completed purchases
3. Revenue / customer value
4. Lead quality
5. Landing or WhatsApp engagement
6. Clicks
7. Impressions

Creative, audience, offer and CTA should be tested as separate variables where practical. Avoid changing multiple major variables simultaneously when evaluating performance.

**Creative relevance rule:** The opening hook, visual, offer and destination must describe the same user value.

**Trust rule:** No fabricated proof, fake scarcity, guaranteed outcomes, deceptive engagement bait, or unsupported claims.

**Fatigue rule:** Monitor frequency, negative feedback and declining qualified-lead rate. Refresh creative when evidence shows fatigue rather than increasing repetition.

**Governance rule:** Optimization must never justify excessive ad density, obstruction of core functions, deceptive placements, or removal of reasonable user control.

## Decision rules

LOW CPL + HIGH SALES -> SCALE  
LOW CPL + LOW SALES -> CHECK LEAD QUALITY / SALES PROCESS  
HIGH CPL + HIGH SALES -> IMPROVE CREATIVE / AUDIENCE  
HIGH CPL + LOW SALES -> PAUSE OR REBUILD

Do not optimize for clicks alone. Optimize for qualified leads, customers and revenue.

## Advertising governance

NORMAL_AD_LOAD → NEVER_EXCEED → PROTECT_CORE_FUNCTIONS → USER_CONTROL

Advertising must remain clearly distinguishable from product content and must not obstruct essential product functions, navigation, creation, messaging, accessibility, security or core workflows.
