What this scanner can and cannot establish

Camera-only limitation: a normal phone camera cannot see through solid materials, detect invisible objects, reveal underground contents, or verify that an object contains treasure. Multi-angle photos can support visual comparison; reliable 3D reconstruction requires calibrated processing and adequate overlapping views.

No images are sent to MercySoul servers by this prototype. No AI diagnosis, material authentication, provenance claim, or treasure detection is performed. Avoid scanning people, private documents, or hazardous objects without appropriate consent and safety precautions.
